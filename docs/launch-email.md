# Tipsite launch email copy

Plain-text copy for the email sent when creator sign-up opens. Replace only the
braced placeholders before sending. `Tipsite` and `tipsite.in` remain product
placeholders, not a final brand or domain.

The `{handle}` sentence is included only when the recipient supplied a handle.
The no-handle version omits it entirely. Each body has one primary CTA:
`Claim your handle: {signup_url}`. The unsubscribe line is a separate required
footer link. In the email template, render the CTA as the only button with label
`Claim your handle` and URL `{signup_url}`.

## English subject lines and preview text

All subject lines are under 45 characters.

| Subject | Preview text |
|---|---|
| Your Tipsite handle can be claimed | Creator sign-up is open. Claim your handle from the waitlist. |
| Creator sign-up is open | Choose your handle and set up your Tipsite creator link. |
| Claim your Tipsite handle | Your creator link can start with the handle you choose. |
| Your creator link starts here | Sign up when you are ready to share one clear tip link. |
| Tipsite sign-up is now open | Claim your handle and keep your creator link close. |

Each plain-text body below is under 150 words, including its footer.

## English email body — handle supplied

```text
Hi,

Creator sign-up is open on Tipsite.

You asked for {handle}. You can now claim it and finish setting up your
creator link.

Claim your handle: {signup_url}

Tipsite keeps the path from your link to a fan's tip simple. 5% of every tip, Razorpay's charges on top.

If now is not the right time, you can come back to this email later.

Why you're receiving this: you joined the Tipsite waitlist.
Remove me from this list: {unsubscribe_url}

{legal_name}
{address}
```
